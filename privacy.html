Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

This policy covers the Dar Ul Uloom Sheffield mobile app, published by Ikhaa Studios. It explains what the app does and does not collect, in plain terms.

The short version

You do not need an account to use this app. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location, and the app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking software.

What the app collects

If you turn on notifications: Firebase Cloud Messaging issues your device a notification token so the masjid can send announcements, event reminders and business offers. This token identifies a device installation, not a person, and we do not link it to any other information. Turning notifications off in Settings unsubscribes the device.

Anonymous view counts: when you open an announcement or event, the app adds one to that post's view counter so the masjid can see roughly how many people saw it. Only the total is stored. No identifier, device token or record of who viewed what is kept, and your device remembers locally which posts it has already counted so the same post is never counted twice.

Your settings stay on your device: text size, dark mode, time format, notification preferences and which posts you have read are all stored locally and are never sent to us.

What the app does not collect

  • No account, sign-in or personal details for ordinary app users.
  • No location data. The app never requests location permission — the directory map shows fixed masjid pins, not your position.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or calendar.
  • No advertising, ad identifiers, analytics or third-party trackers.

The permissions the app requests are limited to internet access, notifications, and the ability to restore your prayer reminders after the device restarts.

Prayer time reminders

Prayer time reminders are created and scheduled entirely on your device from a timetable bundled inside the app. They involve no network connection, no server and no data collection. The prayer timetable itself works fully offline.

Content the app downloads

Announcements, events, the masjid directory and the business directory are read from Google Firebase (Firestore and Storage). This is a one-way, read-only download of public content that anyone using the app can see. As part of serving that request, Google receives standard technical information such as your device's IP address, which is ordinary for any internet service. A copy of the content is cached on your device so it remains readable offline.

The directory map loads its map tiles from OpenStreetMap, which likewise receives your IP address when tiles are requested. Pins are placed from coordinates stored with each masjid listing — your own location is not used.

Who we share information with

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. The only third parties involved are the service providers above — Google (Firebase) and OpenStreetMap — acting to deliver the app's content, each under their own privacy policies.

How long information is kept

Notification tokens are kept only while notifications are switched on; they expire once the app is uninstalled or notifications are turned off. View counts are cumulative totals that contain no personal information. Content cached on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

Children's privacy

This is a general-audience app for the masjid community. It is not directed at children, and because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Masjid and business consoles

Separately from this app, masjid staff and listed businesses use password-protected web consoles to post content and manage listings. Those accounts are created by the masjid and are not part of the app experience — ordinary app users have no account and are not affected.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here and the date at the top will change.
