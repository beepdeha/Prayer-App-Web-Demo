Council of Mosque Sheffield

The prayer times in this app are based on the Council of Mosque Sheffield prayer times. Dar Ul Uloom Sheffield and several masjids around Sheffield — included in the directory — follow the same timetable. Jamaat times may differ.

Business and advertisement

Our mission is to bring the Muslim community closer together and closer to the Masjid and to Islam.

Our Business Directory exists to help our community benefit from the services offered by local Muslim-owned businesses. It's a win for everyone: the community gets easy access to trusted local services, the businesses gain visibility and custom, and every business that advertises with us also directly supports Dar Ul Uloom Sheffield.

For more information, please email farhanfns1@gmail.com.
